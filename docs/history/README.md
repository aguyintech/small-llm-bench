# History

Point-in-time records, kept because they explain *why* parts of the bank look
the way they do. They are not current documentation and were not updated after
the fact, so read the date before trusting a number in any of them.

- `engineering-log.md` — the full development log up to 1.0.0: every bank
  change, the measurement behind it, and the decisions that were later
  reversed. [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md) is the condensed version.
- `v0.11-framework-assessment.md` — independent review across 10 models and 4
  vendor families, when the bank was 52 tasks over 8 modules. File names from
  the recorded sessions it cites are redacted.
- `v0.13-review.md` — what was broken at v0.13, argued from three recorded
  agent sessions against the bench's own numbers.
